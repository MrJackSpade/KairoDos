KairoDos Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

KairoDos is an Android DOS emulator published by Loxifi LLC. This policy covers KairoDos, including the paid Google Play edition and the free GitHub edition. For privacy questions or requests, email admin@loxifi.com.

Data on your device

KairoDos reads the game files and folders that you choose through Android's file picker, and games that another app asks it to open. It uses file contents to identify supported games. Game files, file names, content hashes, settings, and downloaded images stay on your device in the selected folder or the app's private storage. Loxifi does not receive these files, names, hashes, or settings. KairoDos has no account, advertising, analytics, or crash reporting service.

Optional artwork downloads

The standard edition does not bundle artwork. If you choose "Download missing images" or tap an image to request a larger version, KairoDos makes an HTTPS request directly to raw.githubusercontent.com. The requested image URL can reveal which game's artwork was requested. The image service also receives your network IP address and ordinary request information needed to deliver the image. KairoDos does not send your game files, file names, or content hashes to these services, and Loxifi does not receive their request logs. The services control their own logging and retention. Downloaded images are stored on your device.

Catalog updates

When KairoDos checks for catalog updates, it requests the latest catalog over HTTPS from raw.githubusercontent.com, which hosts the Kairo projects' catalogs. The request sends ordinary network information, including your IP address, to that service. It does not send your game files, file names, or content hashes. The hosting service controls its own logs and retention.

Update checks

When the GitHub edition of KairoDos starts, it requests the list of KairoDos's releases over HTTPS from api.github.com to see whether a newer version is available; the Google Play edition does not, since Google Play updates it. The request sends ordinary network information, including your IP address, to GitHub. It does not send your game files, file names, or content hashes. GitHub controls its own logs and retention.

Imported catalog files

When you import a catalog file, a copy of its metadata, artwork and update address is stored privately on your device. Checking installed catalogs contacts only the update addresses supplied by those installed files over HTTPS. Those servers receive your IP address and ordinary request information, but not your game files, library names or content hashes. The servers control their own logs and retention. Removing a catalog deletes its imported metadata and artwork, while keeping your games and personal overrides.

Storage, security, and deletion

KairoDos accesses files only after you select them with Android's file picker or another app asks it to open them. It stores app data in private app storage, does not enable Android app backup, and uses HTTPS for every network request. Local app data remains until you clear KairoDos's storage or uninstall it; that does not remove original game files in your chosen folder. If you use a game's Delete action and confirm, KairoDos deletes that game's file from your chosen folder. Loxifi has no server copy of app data to delete. To ask about data sent to an image or hosting service, contact that service.

Support correspondence

If you email Loxifi for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Email admin@loxifi.com to request deletion of that correspondence.

Changes

We may update this policy when KairoDos's behavior changes. The current version will be available in the app and through the Google Play listing.
