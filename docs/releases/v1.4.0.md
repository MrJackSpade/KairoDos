# v1.4.0

- Every game's D-pad reaches the arrow keys by default. A game's own catalog mapping builds on the all-games mapping instead of replacing it, and only a mapping you save can leave a control unbound (007: Licence to Kill and 369 other games had arrow-less D-pads).
- Your games' mappings follow a cascade: the DOS defaults, your all-games mapping, the game's catalog mapping, then your own for the game.
- Scaling anchor: Settings › Graphics pins the picture to any of nine places on the screen, separately for landscape and portrait, and each game's settings can choose its own. Pick it from a 3x3 grid.
- On a two-screen device (RG DS), the D-pad moves between the screens: down past the last game or the last button on the top screen lands on the bottom screen's first, and up from there comes back.
- On a two-screen device, the bottom screen shows the selected game's page while you browse, then its settings once it is open.
- Artwork prefers LaunchBox's images, and Download missing images reports what it did.
- The library scan is faster, shows its progress, and no longer copies every archive to internal storage to inspect it.
- Settings pages and the flyout scroll with the D-pad, and text fields can open the full-screen keyboard.

Install over v1.3.0 or any v1.3.1 test build to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
